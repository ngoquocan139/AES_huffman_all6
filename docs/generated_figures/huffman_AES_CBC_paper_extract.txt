Abstract 
Biomedical signal processing provides a cross-disciplinary international forum through which research on signal and 
images measurement and analysis of clinical medicine and biological sciences are shared. The broadcast transmission 
in wireless body area networks raises serious security challeng e when employed in biomedical applications. 
Electrocardiography (ECG) signal is frequently used for diagnos is of cardiovascular diseases. However, the ECG 
data need a large memory storage device due to continuous heart  rate logs and vital parameter storage. Thus, 
compression schemes are applied to the ECG data before sending them to telemedicine centers for monitoring and 
analysis. Therefore, a proper compression mechanism cannot only improve the storage efficiency but also help in the 
rapid data transfer from one device to another due to its compa ct size. We propose a scheme using buffer blocks, 
peak detection, compression, and encryption mechanism to enable seamless and secure transmission of ECG signal 
from the sensor to the monitor. The study will prove that the q uality of the reconstructed signals obtained using the 
proposed scheme, which uses discrete wavelet transform, Huffman  coding, and Cipher Block Chaining-Advanced 
Encryption Standard algorithm approach, is superior to that obtained using unencrypted compression. Furthermore, 
the proposed system can provide quality-control compressed data , although the data have been encrypted. The 
experimental results show that the proposed system provides an effective means for assuring data security and 
compression performance for ECG data storage and transmission. The security of the scheme is applied against 
known attacks on privacy, such as eavesdropping and passive monitoring attack.  
Keywords – Compression, DWT, Encryption, Electrocardiogram, Peak Detection, Signal blocks 
1- Introduction 
 Heart-related diseases are becoming more common all over the wo rld due to several factors actively affecting the 
stress level and physical condition of the individuals. In the medical field, electrocardiogram (ECG) signal processing 
was researched numerous times in the past due to its significan ce in monitoring heart status [1]. ECG signals carry 
electrical waves of heart functions, which mainly consist of fi ve kinds of peaks, i.e., P, Q, R, S, and T. The QRS 
complex in this context must be recognized. This condition illu strates left and right ventricular depolarization and 
features a normal duration of 80–100 ms. Normally, a person experiences a heart rate of 80–100 beats per minute. If 
the heart rate exceeds 100 beats per minute, the condition is termed as tachycardia, and if the beats are less than 60 
per minute, bradycardia is diagnosed [2]. 
The Portable ECG sensing devices obtain the signals and send it  directly to the monitor for further analysis, which 
may detect an increase in sheer volume of data in terms of comp lexity and timeliness. Therefore, the signal is

scheme due to different levels of data compressions [8]. Studie s demonstrated that if the efficiency of ECG 
compression scheme is tested alone in terms of quality through PRD method, then variable outcomes may occur. 
Thus, the use of multiple methods (PRD1 and compression ratio ( CR)) was proposed to achieve more accurate and 
reliable results and better conclusions during simulations. Simulations were performed on Massachusetts Institute of 
Technology-Beth Israel Hospital (MIT-BIH) ECG arrythmia dataset with different compression levels to analyze its 
influence on scheme performance using PRD1 and CR. During the p ast few years, various methods have been 
proposed for ECG compression and encryption. Among these method s, lossless methods aim for high compression 
ratio and zero information loss upon reconstruction with a symm etric key block cipher with lightweight properties 
to enhance confidentiality, integrity, and authentication in EC G signal transmission. The privacy issue without 
limiting trading aims to avoid data collection attacks and maintain correct open transaction records [9]. However, the 
amount of privacy protection should be improved when the data transmission size has become extremely large  [10]. 
Hence, most of the previous works concentrated more on the qual ity of signal based on peak detection but lacked 
information on the security aspect. Although several works reported security models with filtering and compression 
schemes, these schemes lacked inefficiency in terms of secure, lossless compression, or noise filtering. We propose 
a new system model based on lossless schemes, such as Huffman c oding for compression, to ensure that no 
information is lost upon reconstruction. To avoid data tamperin g, we use a symmetric ciphering key based on the 
Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)-Cipher Block Chaining (CBC) algorithm with 256-bit key size.

helpful and essential data. Mo st scientists succeed in combinin g encryption and compression. Compression with

transmission rate or low bandwidth in the communication channel . Continuous flow of sign al may lead to packet

could help in this regard.

5- Compression

ratio is calculated to measure the performance of compression s cheme and compactness of data. Two types of 
compression schemes, i.e., lossy and lossless, are available. Lossless schemes recover a compressed file to its original

also affect data aversively. The proposed scheme Huffman coding is used for compression and decompression.

6- Cryptography 
Cryptographic mechanism is a pro cess in which information is co nverted to prevent it from being recognized by 
attackers [33]. The encrypted data are deemed safe for storage status relatively. However, the data encryption process 
still faces adversarial actions [34]. Cryptography includes two processes. The first process is an encryption procedure 
that converts the intelligible data into unintelligible information using a cryptography algorithm and encryption key. 
The second process is decryption, which involves converting the unintelligible data into intelligible information using 
the same algorithm and a decryption key  [35].  Cryptography is  divided into two broad categories: symmetric key 
and asymmetric key cryptography. The first category, also known as symmetric key cryptography (otherwise called 
secret-key cryptography), uses the same key at the source and destination. The second category, that is, asymmetric 
key cryptography, uses different keys (called the public key) at the source and destination [36].

Pan Tompkins method to detect 𝑄𝑅𝑆 peaks count. Further, denoising is performed using wavelets keeping suitable 
threshold, scale and level. Once the denoised signal block i.e.  𝐷𝐹ℬሺ𝚤ሻ෣  is obtained, a lossless compression scheme 
based on Huffman encoding s applied to get compressed block 𝐶𝐹ℬሺ𝑖ሻ. Next, ciphering of block is performed using

ECG signal block and QRS peak detection is achieved through pan Tompkins Scheme. The details of every notation 
and processes involved in this brief algorithm is provided in previous sections.

This work mainly aimed to develop a lightweight model to process ECG signals efficiently and securely. To test the 
efficiency of the proposed model, five distinguished datasets f rom MIT-BIH arrhythmia repository were processed 
through several mechanisms, including denoising, compression, f iltering, and encryption. As compressed signals 
require less computation time compared with raw signals, the Hu f f m a n  l o s s l e s s  s c h e m e  w a s  e m p l o y e d .  T h e

bit AES (CBC), which could be further tested in real-time embedded devices. The compression rate of the algorithm 
is 35.015% and the PRD is 0.411. The reconstructed signal is fu lly reliable with the original signal, and the 
insignificant error between the two signals indicates their sim ilarity. After compression, the ECG signal can be
